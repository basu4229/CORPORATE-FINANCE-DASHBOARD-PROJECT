# Corporate Finance Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Corporate Finance Dashboard designed to provide a clear view of lending performance, profitability, portfolio exposure, and facility-level credit risk.

The dashboard combines financial KPIs, monthly performance trends, profitability analysis, risk indicators, and facility-level details into a single reporting solution.

## Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is structured into three main areas:

- Profitability
- Facility Details
- Client Summary

> Note: The current project documentation focuses on the Profitability and Facility Details views.

---

## Key KPIs

The dashboard provides a high-level view of important financial indicators:

- Loan Book
- Interest Income
- Net Interest Income (NII)
- Net Profit
- Net Interest Margin (NIM)
- Cost of Funds
- Yield on Advance
- Total Loan Exposure
- Provision Amount
- Performing Loan Book
- High-Risk Exposure
- Overdue Principal
- Available Limit
- Days Past Due (DPD)

These KPIs help connect portfolio growth with profitability and credit-risk performance.

## Profitability Bridge

The Profitability Bridge explains how Interest Income moves through major income and expense components to arrive at Net Profit.

The analysis considers:

- Interest Income
- Interest Expense
- Fee Income
- Operating Expense
- Provision Expense
- Tax Expense
- Net Profit

This makes it easier to understand whether changes in profitability are coming from revenue generation, operating costs, provisions, or taxation.

---

## Monthly Performance Analysis

Monthly charts compare:

- Interest Income vs Interest Expense
- Net Interest Margin
- Operating Income vs Operating Expense
- Net Profit Margin

This helps identify periods where margins were under pressure and where income generation improved.

## Key Financial Insights

### Portfolio Growth

The Loan Book closed at approximately ₹20.74B in December, representing around 7.6% growth compared with January.

### Interest Income

December recorded the highest Interest Income at approximately ₹174.23M.

The dashboard indicates that December's Interest Income was around 20.9% higher than February, which was the lowest month.

### September Profitability Pressure

September was the only loss-making month shown in the dashboard.

Net Profit fell to approximately -₹7.46M, with Provision Expense increasing sharply to around ₹76.79M.

This highlights how credit provisions can significantly affect bottom-line profitability.

### Margin Pressure

June showed notable margin pressure.

NIM declined to approximately 4.15%, while Cost of Funds increased to around 6.01%.

This indicates a period where funding costs placed pressure on lending margins.

### Credit Stress

October recorded a significant increase in Overdue Principal, reaching approximately ₹252.90M.

By year-end:

- Around 82% of the Loan Book was performing.
- High-Risk Exposure represented roughly 18% of total loans.

## Facility-Level Risk Analysis

The Facility Details page provides a granular view of individual lending facilities.

Each facility can be evaluated using:

- Facility ID
- Product Name
- Loan Book
- Interest Income
- Net Profit
- Provision Amount
- Risk Grade
- DPD
- Overdue Principal
- Facility Limit

This view helps users move from portfolio-level analysis to individual facility-level investigation.

### Risk Monitoring

Risk Grade and DPD provide quick indicators of credit quality.

The table also highlights Overdue Principal and Facility Limit using visual indicators, making larger exposures easier to identify.

Facilities with higher DPD and overdue balances can therefore be investigated in more detail.

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to demonstrate how Power BI can transform financial and lending data into an interactive management reporting solution.

Instead of looking at individual financial figures separately, the dashboard connects:

**Portfolio → Revenue → Cost → Margin → Profit → Exposure → Credit Risk**

This creates a more complete view of financial performance and helps users investigate the factors behind changes in profitability.

---

## Key Takeaway

The dashboard shows that strong portfolio growth does not automatically translate into stronger profitability.

Interest income, funding costs, operating expenses, provisions, overdue exposure, and credit risk all contribute to the final financial outcome.

The combination of portfolio-level KPIs and facility-level analysis makes it possible to move from:

**"What happened?"**

to

**"Where did it happen?"**

and finally:

**"What is driving it?"**
